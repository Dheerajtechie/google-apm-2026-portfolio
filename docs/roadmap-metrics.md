# Roadmap, Metrics & Experiments

## 2-quarter roadmap

## Q1 — Foundation and trust
- Build ingestion + schema mapping
- Ship theme generation with evidence citations
- Introduce confidence score and review workflow
- KPI target: Insight review completion rate > 70%

## Q2 — Actionability and scale
- Add opportunity scoring and prioritization board
- Ship integrations to Jira/Linear for action handoff
- Launch experiment recommendation templates
- KPI target: Insight-to-action rate > 35%

## KPI tree

### North star
- Insight-to-action rate

### Supporting leading indicators
- Weekly active decision-makers
- Insight acceptance rate
- Median time-to-insight

### Lagging business indicators
- Workspace retention (30/90-day)
- Expansion revenue from growing teams

## Experiment backlog

1. **Confidence UX test**
   - Hypothesis: Showing evidence citations increases PM trust and adoption.
   - Success: +15% insight acceptance rate.

2. **Prioritization model calibration**
   - Hypothesis: Including confidence in scoring improves action conversion quality.
   - Success: +10% experiment win rate.

3. **Onboarding flow simplification**
   - Hypothesis: Guided first-import reduces time-to-first-value.
   - Success: 25% reduction in setup abandonment.

## Decision cadence
- Weekly insight quality review
- Biweekly prioritization council (PM + UXR + Design)
- Monthly metric deep-dive and roadmap adjustment
