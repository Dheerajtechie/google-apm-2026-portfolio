# Product Vision & Strategy

## Vision
Help product teams make better product decisions, faster, by turning scattered user feedback into trusted, actionable insight.

## Strategic thesis
User insight quality is a hidden bottleneck for product velocity. Teams over-index on internal assumptions because customer signal is fragmented, slow to synthesize, and hard to trust.

An AI-powered insights platform can become critical product infrastructure if it delivers:
- **Accuracy:** Themes that reflect user reality.
- **Clarity:** Explainable reasoning behind each recommendation.
- **Actionability:** Direct links to decisions PMs must make this sprint.

## Market framing

### Current alternatives
- Manual spreadsheet tagging and ad hoc dashboards
- Standalone survey tools with weak synthesis
- BI tools that optimize reporting, not product decision velocity

### Opportunity gap
Most tools stop at “what happened.” Teams need help with:
1. Why users behave this way
2. Which problems matter most now
3. What change to test next

## Target segment (beachhead)
- B2B SaaS teams (50–500 employees)
- 3–20 PM/design/research collaborators
- High volume of qualitative + support feedback

## Value proposition
"From feedback chaos to roadmap clarity in hours, not weeks."

## Strategic pillars
1. **Signal unification:** One place for user feedback artifacts.
2. **Insight confidence:** AI summaries with citations + confidence scoring.
3. **Decision acceleration:** Prioritized opportunities with experiment templates.

## Differentiators
- Cross-source synthesis (not siloed analytics)
- Explainability by default (traceability to source evidence)
- PM-native workflow (prioritization and roadmap hooks)

## Business model (initial)
- SaaS subscription by workspace size + usage
- Add-ons for governance, advanced privacy controls, and enterprise support

## 12-month success outcomes
- >35% reduction in time-to-insight
- >20% increase in decision cycle speed (idea → experiment)
- >15% improvement in experiment win rate from better problem selection
